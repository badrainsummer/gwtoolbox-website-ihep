Meaning of catalogue columns
  • m1: mass of the primary star, in unit of solar masses;
  • m2: mass of the secondary star, in unit of solar masses;
  • z: cosmological redshift;
  • D: luminosity distance in Mpc;
  • χ: effective spin;
  • d...: uncertainties of corresponding parameters;
  • dtb: detectability, the probability of detection with the GW detector;
  • inc: the inclination angle of the binary orbital plane.
GRB parameters:
  • Gamma: The bulk Lorentz factor of the jet;
  • dtheta: the half opening angle of the jet;
  • r0: the distance of GRB emission site to the central engine;
  • Egrb: the intrinsic energy released by the GRB;
  • detected: Whether or not is detected by the gamma-ray detector;
  • T90: duration of the burst measured by 90% of the fluence;
  • F_max: maximum flux;
  • t_max: peak time lag with respect to the GW;
  • Epeak: the bending energy in the spectrum;
  • fluence: the energy received by the detector.
